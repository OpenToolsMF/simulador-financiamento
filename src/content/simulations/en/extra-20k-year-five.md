<h2>The scenario</h2>

Both variants start with BRL 300,000, 360 months and SAC. In the second, BRL 20,000 is applied once in month 60 to reduce term.

<h2>How to interpret</h2>

Compare effective term and total interest. The gap after month 60 shows the balance removed and future interest no longer charged on it.

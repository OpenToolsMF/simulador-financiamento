<h2>The scenario</h2>

This base case finances BRL 300,000 for 360 months under SAC. Scheduled amortization is approximately constant and the payment declines with the balance and interest.

<h2>How to interpret</h2>

Use the first payment as the initial peak and sample rows to observe interest being replaced by principal. Total paid is a nominal estimate of the modelled flow.

<h2>The scenario</h2>

One variant follows base SAC. The other adds BRL 500 from month 1, always reducing term.

<h2>How to interpret</h2>

Recurring contributions attack principal early and repeatedly. Compare total contributions, months removed and interest reduction without treating the result as advice.
